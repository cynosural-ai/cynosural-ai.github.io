# Asset sources

`order_policy.png` is a new, transparent, dark-theme rendering of the PyLIMID quickstart’s fitted promotion and order policies. Its plot logic is in `src/app/pylimid-readme/plot_policy.py`. It uses the quickstart’s displayed promotion probabilities, mean order quantities, 0.25-kilogram policy scale, and 0–40-kilogram order domain. The original figure and data are in `docs/_static/figures/quickstart/` in the attached PyLIMID repository: https://github.com/cynosural-ai/pylimid/tree/main/docs/_static/figures/quickstart.

`ice_cream_shop.py` reproduces the runnable ice cream shop example in the PyLIMID README, using its declared model and training settings. Source: https://github.com/cynosural-ai/pylimid#example.

`/pylimid/jax_logo.svg` and `/pylimid/pyro_logo.png` are reused from the PyLIMID brand assets in `public/pylimid`; their sources and licenses are recorded in `public/pylimid/ASSET_CREDITS.md`.
