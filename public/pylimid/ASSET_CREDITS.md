`pyro_logo.png` is the NumPyro logo used in the NumPyro README. Source: https://github.com/pyro-ppl/numpyro. Pyro and NumPyro are distributed under the Apache License 2.0.

`order_policy.png` is a new, transparent, dark-theme rendering of the PyLIMID quickstart’s fitted promotion and order policies. Its plot logic is in `src/app/pylimid/plot_policy.py`. It uses the quickstart’s displayed promotion probabilities, mean order quantities, 0.25-kilogram policy scale, and 0–40-kilogram order domain. The original figure and data are in `docs/_static/figures/quickstart/` in the attached PyLIMID repository: https://github.com/cynosural-ai/pylimid/tree/main/docs/_static/figures/quickstart.

`ice_cream_shop.py` reproduces the runnable ice cream shop example in the PyLIMID README, using its declared model and training settings. Source: https://github.com/cynosural-ai/pylimid#example.
